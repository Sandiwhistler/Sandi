Privacy

Last updated November 2026

This policy covers wearesandi.com and the SANDI app for iPhone, iPad and Android. We keep it simple: we collect as little as possible.

What the app stores

The SANDI app has no accounts and no tracking or advertising tools. Items you save with the heart are stored only on your own device. We never see them, and deleting the app removes them.

Payments

When you buy something, checkout is handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your name, email, shipping address and card details to process the order and shares your order details with us so we can send it to you. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe's privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

How we use your order details

We use them only to fulfil your order, send updates about it, and answer your questions. We don't sell or share them with anyone else.

Email and Instagram

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply. Links to Instagram open Instagram, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

Our shop is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the order information we hold about you at any time.
