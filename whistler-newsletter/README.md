# Whistler Newsletter: Launch Plan

A separate business from SANDI. It's a free weekly newsletter plus an Instagram
account covering what's on in Whistler, written by a local with 8 years in town.
It makes money from local sponsors first, then affiliate links and paid guides.

## The idea in one line

**The Whistler week from someone who actually lives here: what's on, what's
worth it, and where the deals are.**

## The gap we fill

The Whistler newsletters already out there are:

- **Whistler Insider** (Tourism Whistler): official, polished, and promotional.
- **Pique's Friday newsletter**: news-led, sent Friday at around 4pm.
- **Whistler Weekly** (the municipality): civic updates.

I couldn't find an independent, personality-led one. Our angle is **honest
picks, deals and specials, and insider tips**, sent **Thursday morning** so
people can plan the weekend before Pique's Friday email arrives.

## How it makes money

| Stage | Subscribers | Revenue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Months 1–2 | 0–1,000 | "Founding sponsor" spots for local businesses (see `sponsor-pitch.md`) |
| Months 3–6 | 1,000–3,000 | Sponsor slots each week, paid Instagram features, affiliate links (tours, rentals, stays) |
| Month 6+ | 3,000+ | Paid guides ("Whistler for first-timers", "Moving to Whistler"), a local deals directory, and possibly host guidebooks |

**Rough target:** 4 sponsor slots a month at $150–$300 each is $600–$1,200 a
month by months 3–4. That grows as the audience does. This is a goal, not a
promise.

## Who does what

**You (10+ hrs/week)**
- Local knowledge: what's actually good, what's new, gossip-free tips.
- Photos and short videos for Instagram (phone is fine).
- Visiting and messaging businesses to sell sponsor spots. This is the job that
  makes the money.
- Owning the accounts and money, and approving each issue before it sends.

**Me (Claude)**
- Researching that week's events and writing each issue from your notes.
- Captions, post ideas and the content calendar.
- Sponsor pitches, follow-up emails, media kit and invoices.
- A signup landing page, and later a website with guides.
- Monthly look at the numbers (opens, clicks, follower growth) and what to change.

## First 30 days

Whistler Blackcomb's target opening day is **Friday, November 20, 2026**
(depends on snow), which makes a strong reason to launch now.

### Week 1 (Nov 16–22): launch
- [ ] **You:** pick the name (`brand.md`), grab the Instagram handle and the domain.
- [ ] **You:** create a beehiiv account and an Instagram Business account.
- [ ] **Me:** finish issue #1 from your notes (`issue-001-opening-week.md`).
- [ ] **You:** send issue #1 **Thursday, Nov 19** to friends, coworkers and your
      personal Instagram. Ask them to forward it.
- [ ] Post 3–4 times on Instagram around opening day (see `instagram-plan.md`).

### Week 2 (Nov 23–29): first sponsors
- [ ] **You:** list 30 local businesses you know (cafés, après spots, rental
      shops, spas, yoga studios, restaurants).
- [ ] **Me:** personalize the sponsor email for each one.
- [ ] **You:** send 10 and drop into 5 in person. Pitch the founding sponsor deal.
- [ ] Issue #2, Thursday.

### Week 3 (Nov 30–Dec 6): growth
- [ ] Run an Instagram giveaway with a business ("subscribe + tag a friend to win
      a $50 gift card"). It grows the list and gives a sponsor exposure.
- [ ] Put QR code cards in friendly businesses ("Free weekly Whistler guide").
- [ ] Issue #3, Thursday.

### Week 4 (Dec 7–13): review
- [ ] Issue #4, Thursday.
- [ ] **Me:** review the numbers (subscribers, open rate, best links) and adjust.
- [ ] Plan the holiday issues, which are the busiest weeks of the year.

**Day-30 targets:** 300–500 subscribers, 500+ Instagram followers, 1–3
founding sponsors signed.

## Rules we follow
- **Honesty:** sponsored content is always labelled "Sponsored."
- **Trademarks:** don't use Whistler Blackcomb or Vail logos or brand names in
  our branding. Talking about the mountain in writing is fine.
- **Photos:** use your own, or ones businesses give us permission to use.
- **Email:** only send to people who signed up (Canada's anti-spam law, CASL).
  Every issue needs an unsubscribe link, which beehiiv adds automatically.
- **Facts:** check dates and prices before sending. Events move.
