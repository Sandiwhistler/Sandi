# Brand

## Name options

Check the Instagram handle, the domain and BC Registries before you decide.
A quick web search found no Whistler newsletter using any of these.

| Name | Feel | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Stroll** ⭐ | Friendly, local, a little chic | Named after the Village Stroll. Easy to say and remember. "Take a stroll through your Whistler week." |
| **Fitz** | Insider, short | Named after Fitzsimmons Creek. Locals get it, tourists may not. |
| **Two Peaks Weekly** | Clear, touristy | Easy for visitors to understand, less personality. |
| **Après Notes** | Fun, social | Strong for winter, weaker in summer. |
| **Sea to Sky Thursday** | Wider area | Room to grow to Squamish and Pemberton later. |

**My pick: The Stroll.** It works in both seasons, sounds local without
being exclusive, and leaves room for spin-offs like "Stroll Picks" or
"Stroll Deals."

Avoid: "Insider" (Tourism Whistler uses it), and anything with "Whistler
Blackcomb," "Peak 2 Peak" or Vail marks.

## Positioning

**For:** Visitors planning a trip, and locals who want to know what's on.
**Promise:** The best of the week in 5 minutes, chosen by someone who lives
here.
**Different because:** Honest opinions, real deals and hidden spots, not
tourism-board polish.

## Voice
- Like a friend texting you the plan. Warm, a bit cheeky, never snobby.
- Short sentences. Specific picks ("get the soup, sit by the window"), not
  lists of everything.
- Honest. If something's overpriced or overhyped, say so kindly.
- Respect locals and visitors equally. No "tourist" jokes.

## The format of each issue (5-minute read)

1. **The week in one line:** conditions, vibe, the main thing happening.
2. **This weekend:** the 3–5 best events, each with one line on why to go.
3. **The deal:** one special, happy hour or discount worth knowing about.
4. **Local's pick:** one spot, dish, trail or tip, from you.
5. **Sponsored:** one clearly labelled sponsor spot.
6. **Heads up:** road, parking, transit or weather notes.
7. **One last thing:** a photo, a question to readers, or a fun fact.

**Send:** Thursday, 7am Pacific.
