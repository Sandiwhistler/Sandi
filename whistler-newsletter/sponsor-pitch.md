# Sponsors

## Packages

Start with cheap "founding sponsor" spots while the list is small, then raise
prices as it grows.

| Package | What they get | Founding price (first 3 months) | Later price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Featured spot** | Labelled sponsor block in one issue (photo, 50 words, link) | $75 | $150–$300 |
| **Story feature** | One Instagram post + story about the business | $100 | $200+ |
| **Month bundle** | 4 featured spots + 1 Instagram post | $250 | $600+ |
| **Giveaway partner** | They give a prize, we run it, they get the exposure | Free (they provide the prize) | Free or small fee |

**Founding sponsors keep their price for 3 months** after prices go up.
It's an easy yes for them and a reason to sign now.

## Who to approach first

Businesses you already know personally. Rough order:
1. Cafés and bakeries
2. Après bars and restaurants with happy hours
3. Rental and tune shops
4. Spas, yoga and fitness studios
5. Local shops and makers (and SANDI in summer)
6. Tour operators (snowshoeing, sledding, ziplining)

**Tip:** ask for a **deal for readers** (10% off, free coffee with a pastry).
It gives readers a reason to visit, and the business can see that the spot
worked.

## Outreach email

**Subject:** Feature [Business] in Whistler's new weekly email?

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [your name]. I've lived in Whistler for 8 years and just launched
> **The Stroll**, a free Thursday email (and Instagram) about what's on in
> town: the best events, deals and local picks, for locals and visitors.
>
> I'm a big fan of [specific thing you like about them], and I'd love to have
> [Business] as one of our first **founding sponsors**.
>
> - **$75** for a featured spot in one issue (photo, short description, link),
>   or **$250** for a month with an Instagram post included.
> - Founding sponsors keep that price for 3 months after prices go up.
> - Even better if you can add a small deal for readers, so you can see who
>   came in from it.
>
> Happy to pop in and show you the first issue. Does [day] work?
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name]
> [Newsletter link] · [Instagram]

## Follow-up (5 days later)

> Hi [Name], just following up on The Stroll. We're [X] subscribers after
> our first [X] issues and growing every week. I have [1–2] founding spots
> left for December, which is the busiest stretch of the year. Want one?

## After each sponsored issue, send them:
- How many people opened that issue, and how many clicked their link
  (from beehiiv).
- A screenshot of their spot.
- A thank you, and an offer to book the next one.
