About Sandi

Whistler owned. Locally made.

SANDI is a lifestyle fashion brand born and locally made in Whistler, by girls who'd rather be on the mountain. We design for our town: the bike park, the backcountry, the lake days and the après.

Every piece is a love letter to the place we call home, made for girls who go big and still look good doing it.
